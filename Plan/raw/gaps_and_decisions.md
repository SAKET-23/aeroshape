## Physics caveats to state in the final README

- A 2D LBM at Re = 1000 is laminar shedding, not turbulence. The RAE2822 is a transonic airfoil; using it as a target at Re = 1000 and Mach 0.1 is a shape target only, not a statement about its design regime.
- The optimum depends on the constraints. "Recovered NACA0012 / RAE2822" means "converged to a shape close to it under these constraints", and is measured as a distance.
